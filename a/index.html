Utah’s Water Network

Utah's Water Network facilitates statewide coordination and collaboration between diverse partners to solve Utah's water problems.

Utah's Water Network strengthens Utah's capacity to address complex water challenges by convening research, industry, government, Tribal, nonprofit, and community partners to advance science-informed collaboration, innovation, and implementation.

The network's goals are to identify Utah's water sectors and partner groups, identify key water issues, and facilitate solutions by connecting related people and organizations, providing research, technical, policy, and business support, and helping optimize investments.
