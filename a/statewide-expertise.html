Utah's Water Faculty Network

Aligning Faculty Expertise with Utah's Water Challenges

This resource highlights water-related research and expertise among university faculty across Utah.

This resource connects people with water research and expertise across Utah. It helps researchers find collaborators, communities and agencies locate relevant faculty, and partners identify shared priorities and opportunities.

The information comes from UWN faculty surveys and a workshop held in late summer 2026. It shows where water research is happening, which challenges faculty are addressing, and how expertise connects across universities, agencies, communities, and partner organizations.

This is a first version of our data synthesis, and we welcome feedback, ideas, corrections, and collaboration.

AI statement: Our team collected and synthesized the data. AI-assisted tools helped visualize the information on this page.
