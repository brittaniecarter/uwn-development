UWN Events

Join Utah's Water Network for virtual research forums highlighting faculty research on Utah's water challenges. Each forum features brief lightning presentations, followed by questions and group discussion.

All Utah's Water Network members are welcome, regardless of research focus. Join us to learn about ongoing research, connect with colleagues, and explore opportunities for collaboration.
